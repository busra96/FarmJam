# Farm Jam — Merge & Harvest

**Merge matching cards, pack a colorful harvest, and plan every box.**

Welcome to the farm! Combine cards of the same color and number, then drag your upgraded cards onto matching box shapes. Watch your harvest hop into the boxes — and make sure you leave room for the colors still to come.

## How to play

- Drag matching cards together: **1 + 1 → 2**, **2 + 2 → 3**, **3 + 3 → 4**.
- Drop a card onto a transparent shape with the same number of boxes. You choose the color.
- Check the remaining-item counters before committing to a box size.
- Use your limited **Add Card** and **Trash** helpers wisely. Drag a card onto the trash area to discard it.
- Stuck? **Retry** the same level and rethink your plan. Box patterns stay predictable on retries.

## Features

- 35 handcrafted puzzle levels, starting gently and becoming more demanding.
- A colorful farm, satisfying harvest animations, particles and sound.
- First-time tutorial and saved progress.
- English, Turkish, Spanish and Simplified Chinese.
- Ad-free browser edition. No Analytics collection in this edition.

**Controls:** mouse click-and-drag, or touch drag on compatible browsers. Use the gear button for sound and language settings. Fullscreen is available beside the game.

**Browser:** a current desktop browser with WebGL 2 enabled. Audio starts after interacting with the page. Please keep the tab open while playing; progress is stored locally in your browser, not in a cloud account. Private browsing or blocked site storage may prevent saves from persisting.

This is an in-development release. If you find a puzzle problem, please include the level number and a screenshot when leaving feedback.
